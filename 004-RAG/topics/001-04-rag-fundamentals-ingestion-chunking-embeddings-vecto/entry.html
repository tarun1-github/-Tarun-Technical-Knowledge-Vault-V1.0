Embedding

OpenAI's text-embedding-3-large takes text as input and converts it into a list of numbers.

"How do I troubleshoot a Cisco phone?"

↓

text-embedding-3-large

↓

[0.021, -0.034, 0.087, 0.112, ...]

======================================

That list of numbers is called an embedding/vector.

OpenAI's text-embedding-3-large can produce embeddings with up to 3072 dimensions.

A vector of 3072 dimensions means that one piece of text is represented as a list of 3,072 numerical values

3072 dimensions ≠ 3072 words or 3072 pieces of information.

It simply means the numerical representation has 3,072 coordinates.That's a 2-dimensional vector.

Text → [3072 numbers]

###Vector Embedding and vector store DB (Chroma)

The rule to remember

Embedding model dimension = Vector database field dimension

❌ What happens if dimensions don't match?

Suppose your database is configured: dimension = 1536

but your embedding model produces: dimension = 3072

You can't directly store/search that vector.

You'd effectively be trying to put: [3072 values]

into a container designed for: [1536 values]

The vector database will normally return a dimension mismatch/error.
