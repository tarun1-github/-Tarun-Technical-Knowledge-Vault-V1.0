4) Orchestrator–Workers Workflow

A central LLM (Orchestrator) dynamically decides what subtasks are required, sends them to worker nodes/agents, and then a Synthesizer combines their results.

A. The Orchestrator is the central LLM.

It receives the user's request and decides:

What subtasks are needed?

How many workers are needed?

What should each worker do?

What information should be given to each worker?

Worker 1 → Analyze infrastructure

Worker 2 → Analyze applications

Worker 3 → Analyze security

Worker 4 → Analyze databases
